## Executive result

All four candidates remain capable of satisfying the clarified personal-CRM outcome on paper. None has a current S-01 through S-08 `Conflict`. The weighted comparison recommends these two finalists:

1. **OnePageCRM Professional** - strongest match to the required next-action discipline and lowest documented paid two-user cost.
2. **Capsule Starter** - strongest mobile/offline, opportunity-linked task, relationship-history, and exit evidence, at a higher cost.

Less Annoying CRM and Bigin Premier remain paper viable but are not recommended because CP-010 permits no more than two finalists. LACRM ranks behind Capsule on mobile and opportunity-specific task evidence. Bigin ranks fourth because the directly observed setup and daily workflow appear more administratively involved than the clarified personal outcome requires.

### Ranking rationale

- **OnePageCRM leads** because the Action Stream operationalizes the exact next-action habit, the Professional tier covers the simple pipeline and mobile loop, and its annual two-user cost is lowest.
- **Capsule is second** because offline-capable mobile use, opportunity-linked Tasks, record histories, and stronger export evidence reduce operational and exit risk. Its higher cost keeps it behind OnePageCRM.
- **LACRM is third** because its one-tier simplicity and task visibility are strong, but browser-only mobile evidence and Contact-scoped Tasks create more uncertainty for a customer with multiple active Opportunities.
- **Bigin is fourth** because it is capable and affordable, but the direct configuration record shows more setup and daily-view complexity than the clarified personal outcome should require.

## Approved finalists - D-117

### 1. OnePageCRM Professional

The practical session should confirm only the evidence gaps that matter to Loryn: mobile creation from an independently received lead, last-name and phone-number lookup, note/history review, completing and rescheduling a Next Action, today's/overdue work, and moving one Deal through the small pipeline. It should also expose whether Contact-centered Next Actions remain understandable when one customer has more than one Opportunity.

### 2. Capsule Starter

The practical session should confirm the same core loop, with particular attention to whether adding a Contact, Opportunity, and linked Task is fast enough; whether phone/last-name lookup is obvious; and whether offline-to-online synchronization is understandable. The session does not need to test integrations, automation, advanced reporting, or multiple pipelines.
