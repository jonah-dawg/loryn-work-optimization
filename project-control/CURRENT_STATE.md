## Current product outcome

Configure a simple, mobile-capable personal CRM that Loryn will consistently use to track independently sourced leads and prospects, especially those received through email and text. The personal CRM is the record of truth for those independent relationships, their history, status, next action, and due date through a won or lost/archive decision.

This is not a complete order-management, installation, field-service, or Centah-replacement project.

## Approved source-of-truth boundary

| Work or information | Authoritative system |
|---|---|
| Independently sourced leads and prospects | Personal CRM |
| Independent contact details, notes, activities, status, next action, and due date | Personal CRM |
| Independent quote/decision status through `Won` or `Lost / Archived` | Personal CRM |
| Costco-originated lead and job processing, governed identifiers, and Costco-required actions | Centah |
| Optional awareness of a Costco lead in the personal CRM | Minimal manual reference or status note only |

- Centah remains the record of truth for Costco-originated work.
- A Centah integration, API, automatic synchronization, duplicate-control mechanism, or full CRM mirror is not required for personal-CRM selection.
- A manual extract, manual reference, or no cross-system copy is acceptable.
- Independently sourced business is not required to enter Centah.
- If a Costco reference is placed in the personal CRM, it must not override Centah-governed information.

## Initial personal-CRM lifecycle

The initial product needs only a small pipeline equivalent to:

1. `New`.
2. `Contacting`.
3. `Appointment Scheduled`.
4. `Quote / Decision`.
5. `Won`.
6. `Lost / Archived`.

Every active independent lead or prospect requires a next action and due date unless it is explicitly closed, archived, or in a documented exception state.

The initial CRM does not need to manage internal order handoff, DocuSign, installation checks, three-month installation exceptions, post-install support, or repair cases. CP-002 and CP-003 preserve those broader workflow designs as possible future expansion context, but they are not initial selection or configuration requirements after CP-010.
