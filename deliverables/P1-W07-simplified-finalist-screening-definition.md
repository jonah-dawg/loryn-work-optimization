## Screening method

### Minimum-fit review

Each S-01 through S-08 result must use one of these labels:

- `Supported` - current evidence directly or officially establishes a practical fit.
- `Conditional` - the candidate appears capable, but a material limit or bounded confirmation remains.
- `Unverified` - available evidence cannot support a conclusion.
- `Conflict` - current evidence shows that the candidate prevents the clarified personal outcome, creates unacceptable data-loss or access risk, or makes routine use disproportionate.

Evidence must also be labeled `Direct`, `Official`, or `Inference`, dated where known, and assigned `High`, `Medium`, or `Low` confidence. Direct evidence controls when it conflicts with marketing or documentation. Missing evidence remains missing; it is not scored as failure merely because another product has more documentation.

A candidate is `Paper viable` only when every minimum-fit requirement is either `Supported` or `Conditional`, all conditional items have a bounded and proportionate confirmation path, current two-user cost and meaningful limits are documented, and no `Conflict` remains. A candidate is `Incomplete` when material evidence gaps prevent that conclusion. A candidate is `Not advanced` only when a conflict is direct or sufficiently authoritative and material under CP-010.

### Weighted comparison

Only paper-viable candidates receive a weighted comparison. Rate each dimension from 0 to 5:

| Rating | Meaning |
|---:|---|
| 0 | Prevents the clarified outcome or creates unacceptable risk |
| 1 | Major routine barrier |
| 2 | Material weakness or administrative burden |
| 3 | Acceptable for the initial personal workflow |
| 4 | Strong, simple fit |
| 5 | Exceptional fit supported by strong evidence |

Calculate each contribution as `rating / 5 x dimension weight` and total the five contributions out of 100. The weighted total ranks acceptable products; it cannot override an S-01 through S-08 conflict or substitute for missing evidence.

| Dimension | Weight | Decision focus |
|---|---:|---|
| Everyday mobile and desktop usability | 35 | Capture, lookup, note/history review, next-action update, and ordinary navigation |
| Follow-up and next-action reliability | 25 | Today/overdue tasks and a sustainable rule for keeping active records actionable |
| Contact history, search, and simple pipeline | 20 | Reusable relationship record, understandable history, search, lifecycle, and outcomes |
| Cost and administration burden | 10 | Likely two-user production cost, tier limits, setup burden, and routine maintenance |
| Access, export, and basic production readiness | 10 | Two approved users, dependable saving, basic security, export/exit, and acceptable limits |

If totals are close, prefer the product with the simpler routine next-action practice, lower administration burden, clearer two-user production boundary, and less need for confirmation. Feature breadth outside CP-010 is not a tie-breaker.

## Stopping rules

- Stop and mark a candidate `Not advanced` when current direct or authoritative evidence establishes a CP-010 conflict.
- Stop and mark a candidate `Incomplete` when the only remaining proof requires an account, trial, tenant, billing, purchase, private data, or product interaction not separately authorized.
- Stop before using a higher tier's evidence for a lower tier or allowing trial-only features to contaminate a production-tier conclusion.
- Stop before adding custom code, paid add-ons, integrations, or external automation to make a candidate appear viable.
- Stop before a third finalist; if more than two candidates are viable, rank them under the approved weights and recommend the strongest two for review.
- Stop immediately if any source or artifact would expose credentials, private account information, real customer data, or vendor sample identities.
