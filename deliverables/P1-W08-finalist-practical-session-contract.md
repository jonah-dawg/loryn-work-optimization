## Acceptance and decision rules

A finalist is `Practically acceptable` only when:

- all six participant tasks can be completed in the intended session without a material save or access failure;
- last-name and phone-number lookup work on the phone;
- today and overdue work are understandable;
- notes and history remain understandable after updates;
- every active fixture ends with a next action and due date;
- the pipeline status can be changed without losing history;
- ordinary use does not require an integration, automation, paid add-on, or disproportionate duplicate entry; and
- Loryn says she would use the product, with or without stated reservations.

A material blocker is any failure that prevents the clarified CP-010 outcome, creates unacceptable save or history uncertainty, or makes routine use disproportionate. If both products are acceptable, prefer Loryn's stated choice; if she has no preference, use the P1-W07 ranking and administration/cost evidence as the tie-breaker. If neither is acceptable, stop before selecting a CRM and define the smallest reserve-candidate confirmation needed.

## Stopping rules

Stop the setup or session immediately when:

- billing, purchase, a paid activation, or an unapproved add-on is required;
- the available trial cannot be distinguished from the named production tier well enough for fair evidence;
- the product requires real data, an external connection, or a sent communication to complete a core task;
- private credentials, MFA details, recovery details, notifications, or non-synthetic data could enter an artifact or recording;
- a login, device, network, or accessibility issue makes the result unfair; reschedule rather than score the interruption as a product failure;
- the session reaches 20 minutes before the core loop is complete; record the stopping point without extending Loryn's burden; or
- Loryn asks to stop.
