# P1-W02 - Next CRM Comparison Contract

**Status:** Unapproved - proposed outcome and boundaries only

**Phase:** Phase 1 - Zoho prototype and HubSpot comparison

**Updated:** July 31, 2026

**Last signed-off checkpoint:** CP-004

## Proposed work-unit outcome

Define and approve the evidence-first screening contract for the next CRM candidate, beginning with the planned HubSpot comparison, so the project can determine whether a practical production tier can represent the CP-003 workflow before creating another account or involving Loryn.

This outcome is proposed, not approved.

## Starting evidence

- CP-004 supplies the mandatory gates, weights, fixtures, scenarios, evidence format, and participant-burden limit.
- P1-W01 established that Zoho Free cannot represent the approved field model and retained Zoho Professional only as a documentation-only possibility.
- Centah remains a nonselectable Costco-side system and current-state baseline, never the final standalone CRM.
- The next candidate must support independent prospects and jobs and an evidence-backed manual bridge or future integration path for Costco/Centah work.

## Proposed contract boundaries

- Review current official feature, tier, administration, MFA, export, retention, API, mobile, and production-cost evidence before authorizing an account.
- Identify the lowest plausible production tier for the approved CP-003 fields and relationships; do not treat a Free label as proof of production viability.
- Use the same CP-004 gates, synthetic fixtures, and scenario definitions so results remain comparable.
- Keep account creation, configuration, fixture entry, and testing behind separate explicit action gates.
- Do not assign setup, evidence capture, reset work, or homework to Loryn. She tests only later finalists under the approved burden cap.
- Do not use real data, connect email, calendar, maps, Centah, or another external service, send messages, enter billing information, start a paid trial, purchase anything, or select a platform.
- Stop before account creation if official evidence confirms that no reasonable tier can meet a mandatory gate.

## Proposed first decision

Approve or revise the proposed P1-W02 outcome before drafting the detailed evidence checklist, account procedure, testing sequence, and action-gate phrases.

Approval of this outcome will authorize contract drafting and official-evidence research only. It will not authorize a HubSpot or other external account.
