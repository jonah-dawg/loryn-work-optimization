## Recommendation

Advance Freshsales Pro to complete-contract review. Pro is the lowest currently documented Freshsales tier that plausibly fits the approved source-specific workflow because:

- Pro permits 250 custom fields per module, well above the CP-003 baseline.
- Pro supports up to 10 deal pipelines and 100 field dependencies per entity.
- A dependent field can be required only when its controlling choice applies, which may support Costco/Centah-only handoff fields without requesting them for Independent jobs.
- Deal custom fields may be unique, and Freshsales documents unique-value validation for manual saves, bulk updates, APIs, and CSV imports.
- The Activities dashboard combines tasks, meetings, custom activities, due dates, open work, and overdue work; the mobile home combines a calendar, tasks, appointments, and overdue tasks.
- Workflows can create dated tasks from record dates and can run on contacts, deals, appointments, and tasks.
- Full CRM export, CSV/XLSX import, mobile offline records, and REST APIs are documented.

Freshsales Pro is not yet viable or preferred. The official material does not establish one practical action center containing all five M-01 categories, and a fully loaded trial can expose higher-tier features. Direct evidence is mandatory before any pass or score.

## Why Pro is the approved candidate boundary

- Growth provides 150 custom fields but only one pipeline and does not retain active field dependencies after downgrade.
- Pro provides 250 custom fields, 10 pipelines, field dependencies, custom sales activities, and more advanced workflow/reporting capacity.
- Enterprise adds field-level permissions, sandbox, custom modules, and audit logs at a materially higher cost. Those features are not presumed necessary for this one-person operation; any Enterprise dependency must be demonstrated against a mandatory gate rather than adopted for feature breadth.
- No add-on, Freshsales Suite marketing function, marketplace app, custom module, or custom application may be used to make Pro appear to pass.

## Current cost and trial boundary

- Current official US list pricing shows Pro at `$39` per user per month billed annually.
- The two-administrator annual baseline is `$78` per month equivalent or `$936` per year before tax and optional add-ons.
- Freshworks' published price list identifies a `$47` monthly-billed Pro figure, making the indicative two-administrator monthly baseline `$94` per month; exact current checkout must be verified before any paid decision.
- Enterprise is `$59` per user per month billed annually, or `$1,416` per year for two administrators, if a later mandatory-gate finding proves it necessary.
- The Freshsales trial lasts 21 days, requires no credit card according to official support material, and begins as a fully loaded CRM. If no plan is selected and paid at expiry, Freshworks says the account is suspended.
- A later contract must define how Pro-equivalent evidence will be isolated from the fully loaded trial before configuration begins.

## Decision-critical risks

### 1. One-screen morning action center

Official evidence supports actionable Activity dashboard lists, today/overdue filters, a mobile calendar, and overdue tasks. It does not yet prove one practical screen that simultaneously shows today's appointments, people to contact today, overdue actions, jobs waiting on someone else, and active records missing a next action or due date. M-01 therefore remains `Unverified` and is the first direct stopping test.

### 2. Source-specific enforcement and import integrity

Field dependencies can conditionally require Costco/Centah-only fields in record forms. Freshsales also states that import and bulk-update operations do not validate dependency matches, so mismatched source-specific values may be saved. A unique Deal field is separately documented as validating manual, bulk, API, and CSV saves. The contract must test both controls together and must not mistake uniqueness for complete source correctness.

### 3. Trial contamination

The trial is described as fully loaded rather than Pro-only. Every configured or tested feature must have independent Pro-tier evidence. If a clean Pro-equivalent boundary cannot be established without billing or a higher paid tier, the run stops before configuration.

### 4. Administration and history

Freshsales documents account administrators, custom roles, record scopes, user deletion, export history, and 90-day configuration audit logs. Current pricing identifies audit logs and field-level permissions as Enterprise features. Direct evidence must determine whether Pro's ordinary record/activity history is sufficient for the right-sized M-05 requirement or whether an unacceptable Enterprise dependency remains.

### 5. Cost and maintenance

Pro's documented two-administrator annual baseline is `$936`, materially above Bigin Premier and the previously screened entry tiers. A result cannot be preferred unless its mandatory-gate performance and daily usability justify that recurring cost and the expected configuration can be maintained within the approved roughly 15-minute weekly administration limit.

### 6. Search, mobile timing, directions, and sync

Official documentation supports global name/title search, mobile records and activities, quick add, voice notes, and offline create/cache/sync. Phone-number and last-name search, driving directions, one-minute task timing, conflict behavior, and visible recovery from weak-signal sync must be tested directly.
